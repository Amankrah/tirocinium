# 0096: a writing pad with tools

Decision 0042 gave the handwriting sheet a pen and a control that clears the whole page. That is enough to make a mark and not enough to correct one: a student who miswrites a symbol either lives with it or throws the page away, and raw pointer samples come out jagged, which is a poor thing to hand to a reader of handwriting. The pad now keeps its ink as strokes. A pen, an eraser, and undo and redo act on those strokes, with three line weights and pressure when the pointer reports it. The stylus eraser tip erases without a mode change. A palm-sized touch, or a finger that lands while a stylus is down, is ignored, and a pointer the system cancels does not become ink. Ruled lines are painted over the sheet as a guide and are not part of the exported page, so the marker still receives ink on white. One ink colour stays, and the file modes stay the way in for anyone without a pointer.
