# 0086: a chain of equalities breaks at the equals

Display math in the reading column scrolls rather than widening the page. A worked solution states a long derivation as one formula, several equalities on a single line, and KaTeX will not wrap it. Centred in a column narrower than the formula, the left side sits where a scroll cannot reach and the last power is cut off, so a student can read a different equation from the one that was stored. The reading surfaces now break a display formula at each top-level equals and align the lines. The break is applied to the formula text KaTeX is about to read. Writing the line breaks back into the markdown lets the parser eat them, and the formula is then shown as its own source. An equals inside a group, a single relation, and inline math are left as written. This is a display decision: the stored solution is not rewritten, and the text offered to the tutor is still the professor's source.
