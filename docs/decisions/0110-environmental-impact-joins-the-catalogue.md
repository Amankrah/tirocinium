# 0110: Environmental impact joins the catalogue

Date: 2026-10-26. Backend and web, marketplace (decisions 0076, 0100, 0108).

The environmental layer is catalogue v3 of bree-216, derived from v2 by `scripts/revise_catalogue_v3.py`, which reads the indicator table in the BREE 216 Marketplace 2 teaching page and overlays it on the lines that already exist. It adds cradle-to-gate carbon and water, a realistic end-of-life recovery kept distinct from the property-sheet recyclability, the lowest useful service temperature, an end-of-life note, and, for a process, energy and carbon per unit of process, because a process has no mass. Comparison rows, lecture bands, per-unit figures and the three carbon-weighted indices (density times carbon over yield, density times carbon over the square root of modulus, and carbon per dollar) are computed on the server, and a quotation sums the same per-unit figures, with road-freight carbon stated separately from the cradle-to-gate total. A missing figure stays null, and a published zero stays zero. v1 and v2 are unchanged, a course pinned to either stays there until it is re-pinned, and an unpinned project seat receives v3 because it is the newest shipped version.
