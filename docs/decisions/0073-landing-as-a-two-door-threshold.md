# 0073: The landing as a two-door threshold

Date: 2026-09-14. Milestone 9.6 (web). Author: frontend engineer.

Decision 0072 gave the landing two quiet doors in a header, which served entry
but told a cold visitor nothing and addressed neither audience. The guides fix
the hero (wordmark, tagline, the Roman line told once, the particle field as
the one signature moment) but are silent on what else a landing may say, so
this records it. The doors move from the header into the hero as two addressed
cards, the student's first because a code card in hand is the dominant arrival,
with the accent spent on that single action; the professor's card offers sign
in and create an account side by side. Below the fold the page states the
product honestly in the copy voice of guide 3.4: the three-step practice loop,
one italic line owning the calm position of guide 4.2b (no streaks, no nudges,
nothing infinite), and four plain claims for professors (figures stay pixels,
independent verification, seats not accounts, no ranking), each of which is a
tested product property rather than a promise. Everything below the hero is
server-rendered markup on the token layer, hairline rules carry the structure,
motion stays functional hover feedback under the motion tokens, and the close
repeats the two doors so the calls to action bracket the page.
