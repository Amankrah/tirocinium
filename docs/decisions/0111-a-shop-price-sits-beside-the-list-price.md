# 0111: A shop price sits beside the list price

Date: 2026-10-26. Backend and web, marketplace (decisions 0076, 0100).

A published price from a real shop may be shown next to a catalogue line, and it may not become that line. `catalogue/bree-216/market-sources.json` names, for a handful of stocked grades, one public Shopify product page, the exact cut on that page, and a sentence saying what that cut is, because the shop's size is not the line's size. `scripts/refresh_market_checks.py` reads each page's machine-readable product document, keeps the named variant's price in cents, and writes `$TIRO_DATA_DIR/market-checks.json`. A page that does not answer, or no longer carries that cut, leaves the previous cents in place and marks the reading stale; a line that has never been read successfully has no reading. The project line page shows the figure in CAD, the same figure with the catalogue's GST and QST added, the day, the shop and that sentence, and says the catalogue price above is before tax and is the one a quotation uses. The taxed shop figure is not written into `list_price_cents`, is not an input to `compute_quote`, is not put on the supplier fact sheet, and is absent from the case-study line, whose price is the seeded one. A weekly timer runs the script. Lines with no mapped page, including every process, stay unchecked.
