# 0111: A shop price sits beside the list price

Date: 2026-10-26. Backend and web, marketplace (decisions 0076, 0100).

A published price from a real shop may be shown next to a catalogue line, and it may not become that line. `catalogue/bree-216/market-sources.json` names, for a handful of stocked grades, one public Shopify product page, the exact cut on that page, and a sentence saying what that cut is, because the shop's size is not the line's size. `scripts/refresh_market_checks.py` reads each page's machine-readable product document, keeps the named variant's price in cents, and writes `$TIRO_DATA_DIR/market-checks.json`. A page that does not answer, or no longer carries that cut, leaves the previous cents in place and marks the reading stale; a line that has never been read successfully has no reading. The project line page shows the shop, the day and the published price, and the length that price is for. When that same page published a photograph of the product, the line shows it and names the shop, and the catalogue card shows the same photograph. It is shown only when the shop sells that grade and that section. A different thickness, a different form, or a different condition is not mapped. The published price is not written into `list_price_cents`, is not an input to `compute_quote`, is not put on the supplier fact sheet, and is absent from the case-study line, whose price is the seeded one. A weekly timer runs the script. Lines with no mapped page, including every process, stay unchecked.
